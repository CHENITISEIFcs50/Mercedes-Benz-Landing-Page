Heritage

Nearly a century under the three-pointed star

Mercedes-Benz traces its roots to the pioneering work of Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler in the 1880s, working independently just kilometers apart on the earliest gasoline-powered vehicles. Their two companies merged in 1926 to form Daimler-Benz, uniting the three-pointed star with the Benz laurel wreath into the emblem the world recognizes today.

For nearly a century since, the brand's philosophy has stayed the same: "Das Beste oder nichts" — the best or nothing. From the first Motorwagen to today's AMG performance models and Maybach flagships, every Mercedes-Benz is built on that same pursuit of excellence.
